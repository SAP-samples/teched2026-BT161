# Exercise 1 — The Governance Dashboard

## Overview

SAP AI Agent Hub is your command center for AI governance. Before taking any action, you need to understand what is happening in your landscape right now. The dashboard gives you that picture at a glance.

**Duration:** 10 minutes

## Background

AI Agent Hub continuously discovers agents across your connected SAP and third-party environments — including SAP standard agents, agents built with Joule Studio, and agents running in external systems. When something new appears, it surfaces on your dashboard and in your inbox so you can act on it.

## Step 1 — Review the Dashboard

1. On the AI Agent Hub home screen, open the **Dashboard**.
2. Take a moment to read the overview. You should see metrics covering:
   - Total agents, MCP servers, and LLMs in the inventory
   - Agents by verification status (verified, unverified, pending)
   - Agents by risk classification
   - Recent governance activity

## Step 2 — Spot the New Discoveries

1. Look for the **Newly Discovered** section or indicator on the dashboard.
2. You should see **3 new agents** have been discovered since your last session.
3. Note their names — you will focus on one of them in the exercises that follow.

> **What "discovered" means:** These agents were found automatically by AI Agent Hub scanning your connected systems. They exist and are potentially running — but they have not yet been reviewed, owned, or approved by your governance team.

## Step 3 — Understand the Inventory Scope

1. Navigate briefly to the **Inventory** view.
2. Use the **Type** filter to see agents grouped by category:
   - **SAP Standard Agents** — delivered by SAP, part of your licensed solutions
   - **Custom Agents** — built with Joule Studio by teams in your organization
   - **Third-Party Agents** — running in external environments, connected via MCP
3. Return to the Dashboard when you are done exploring.

## Summary

You now have a clear picture of your AI landscape — including 3 agents that need governance attention. In Exercise 2, you will open the inbox, investigate the most relevant discovery, and decide whether to bring it into your governed inventory.
