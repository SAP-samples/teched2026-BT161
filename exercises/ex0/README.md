# Getting Started

## Overview

Before the exercises begin, log in to both systems and take a few minutes to orient yourself.

**Duration:** 10 minutes

## Systems You Will Use

| System | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| SAP AI Agent Hub (shared) | Your command center for AI governance across the organization |
| Joule Studio (individual) | Your AI assistant — only verified agents are allowed to run here |

## Step 1 — Log In to SAP AI Agent Hub

1. Open your browser and navigate to the **AI Agent Hub URL** provided on your session card.
2. Sign in with the shared tenant credentials provided.
3. You should land on the **AI Agent Hub home screen**.

## Step 2 — Log In to Joule Studio

1. Open a new browser tab and navigate to your individual **Joule Studio URL** (provided on your session card).
2. Sign in with your **SAP Universal ID**.
3. Confirm the Joule Studio interface loads. You will return here in Exercise 6.

## Step 3 — Quick Orientation in AI Agent Hub

Spend two minutes exploring the interface:

- The **Dashboard** is your starting point — it gives you a real-time view of your AI governance posture.
- The **Inventory** shows all agents, MCP servers, and LLMs known to the system.
- The **Inbox** surfaces governance actions that need your attention — new discoveries, pending reviews, and alerts.
- The **AI Governance Assistant** (or EA Assistant) is available throughout the session to help you query your landscape.

> **Do not make any changes yet.** Exercise 1 starts from the Dashboard.
