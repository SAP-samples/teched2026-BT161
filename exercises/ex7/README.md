# Exercise 7 (Optional) — Governance Posture & Reporting

## Overview

Governance is only effective if you can measure and communicate it. In this optional exercise you will review the governance dashboard in SAP AI Agent Hub, understand your current coverage posture, and explore how to generate a summary for stakeholders.

**Duration:** 10 minutes

## Background

After working through discovery, assessment, ownership, verification, and decommissioning, your organization's AI governance posture has improved measurably. But the work is never done — new agents are deployed constantly, verified agents drift out of compliance, and the regulatory environment evolves. The governance dashboard helps you track progress and identify where to focus next.

## Step 1 — Open the Governance Dashboard

1. In AI Agent Hub, navigate to the **Dashboard** or **Governance Overview** section.
2. Review the summary metrics:
   - Total agents discovered
   - Agents with owners assigned (% coverage)
   - Agents by risk classification
   - Verified vs. unverified breakdown
   - Agents pending assessment

## Step 2 — Identify Coverage Gaps

1. Look for the segment of agents that are:
   - Discovered but not yet assessed
   - Assessed but not yet owned
   - Owned but not yet verified
2. These represent your governance backlog — the work remaining after today.

> **Think about it:** If this were your real organization, which gap would you prioritize first? Ownership (accountability) or verification (enforcement)?

## Step 3 — Export a Summary

1. If the dashboard supports export, generate a summary report (PDF, CSV, or equivalent).
2. Review what is included: agent names, risk levels, owners, verification status.
3. Consider: who in your organization would receive this report, and how often?

## Step 4 — Reflect on the Full Journey

Look back at what you accomplished in this workshop:

| Exercise | Action | Governance Value |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Discovery | Visibility — you now know what exists |
| 2 | Assessment | Accountability — you evaluated risk |
| 3 | Ownership & Classification | Control — someone is responsible |
| 4 | Verification | Enforcement — compliant agents are approved |
| 5 | Joule Studio enforcement | Runtime — governance affects the user experience |
| 6 | Decommissioning | Closure — non-compliant agents are removed |
| 7 | Reporting | Measurement — posture is visible and communicable |

## Summary

You have completed the full AI governance lifecycle inside SAP AI Agent Hub — from shadow AI discovery to decommissioning and reporting.
